The Secret Guide

You found the hidden path. Every trigger, effect, and little rule of the kaomojiserv Easter eggs is listed here.

Test seasons out of season

Seasonal Easter eggs appear automatically according to your browser's local calendar. To test one immediately, you can force a season:

  • Easter
  • April Fools
  • Halloween
  • Christmas
  • New Year
  • Seasons off

The natural schedule is: April 1, Halloween in October, Christmas from December 1 to 26, New Year from December 28 to January 2, and Easter from Easter Sunday through the following six days. The preview and the date calculation use your browser's local time zone.

01 · EASTER EGG HUNT

Three eggs in the margins

Open /?season=easter and look for the three small symbols in the page margins:

  1. ◇ top left, at about 7 vw and 16 vh
  2. ◯ right, at about 8 vw and 39 vh
  3. ⬡ bottom left, at about 21 vw and 12 vh

Click all three of them. Found eggs turn fainter and smaller; once all are found, the bunny (\/) (•ㅅ•) (\/) appears and the button reads HOP.

Progress lives in sessionStorage under season-easter-eggs and season-easter-complete. Clear these entries to reset the hunt in the same browser session.

02 · BUNNY SHORTCUT

The secret word

In easter mode you can skip the hunt and simply type bunny. It completes the egg hunt instantly and triggers the same bunny animation.

03 · APRIL FOOLS

An AI with emotional bandwidth

On the first visit to /?season=april-fools in a session, AI-enhancing emotional bandwidth... appears with an intentionally fake loading bar. Afterward the page briefly flips upside down and switches to Comic Sans.

The intro state lives in sessionStorage under season-april-intro. Clear it to see the prank again. The footer also randomly shows one of three corporate slogans.

04 · APRIL FOOLS ESCAPE

The fleeing YOINK button

In April Fools mode, move a pointer over YOINK. On the first attempt the button dodges with NICE TRY. On the second attempt it surrenders with YOINK (I SURRENDER).

This only works with a non-touch pointer and is stored as season-april-dodge in sessionStorage. With reduced motion there is no dodging; it surrenders right away.

05 · HALLOWEEN

The ghost in the kaomoji

In Halloween mode you can press and hold the displayed kaomoji for 900 ms without moving the pointer away. Alternatively, typing boo works too.

The page flickers briefly, a ghost ～(つˆДˆ)つ｡☆ appears, and the countdown says BOO!. No further haunt starts during the 2.7-second scare. At midnight the footer also reads hold still... something is near.

06 · CHRISTMAS

Unwrapping the gift

Open /?season=christmas and click [ unwrap gift ] or type hohoho.

Then lights, falling holiday symbols, a Santa hat, and the gift gift: *<|:-) appear. The unwrapped state persists across season-christmas-unwrapped in sessionStorage.

07 · NEW YEAR

Fireworks at the turn of the year

In New Year mode the button counts down to midnight. You can click the countdown or type the upcoming target year to launch the fireworks immediately. At midnight they start automatically.

Four firework waves reveal (ﾉ◕ヮ◕)ﾉ*:･ﾟ✧ YEAR. The automatic celebration runs only once per year, stored in localStorage under season-new-year-YEAR. A click can still run it again. In a New Year preview outside of December or January, the countdown is only ten seconds in the future.

08 · EXPRESSIVENESS OVERLOAD

The secret rave

The rave works in any season, including with /?season=none. There are two triggers:

  1. Type the Konami code: ↑ ↑ ↓ ↓ ← → ← → B A
  2. Click the footer exactly seven times

For ten seconds the background colors cycle, the kaomoji bounces and shifts color, and ☆, ♡, and ツ rain across the page. The footer shows ✦ EXPRESSIVENESS OVERLOAD ✦ meanwhile.

Little rules

  • prefers-reduced-motion: reduce reduces or suppresses particles and motion.
  • sessionStorage applies to the current browser tab or session; localStorage persists beyond it.
  • If a typed word does not respond, the browser window needs keyboard focus and no modifier key like Ctrl, Alt, or Cmd may be pressed.
